v1.0.35
- What's new (this list) in Settings > Updates and in the installer.
- Documents open in their own windows, so you can have several open at once.
- Versions show the bug fix number (for example 1.0.35.1). The Updates section says "installed" correctly for a copy put in place by the installer.
- Update check shows the real reason when it cannot reach GitHub. The auto-update option has shorter wording.
- In full screen Now mode you get a notification that an update is ready instead of an automatic update.

v1.0.34 (new installer and updates)
- Settings has an Updates section: version, check now, update now, and an option to update automatically when you are not using Noctua.
- Noctua checks for updates every 10 minutes and tells you when one is available. Auto-update restarts Noctua by itself and shows "Update complete".
- In full screen Now mode you get a notification instead of an automatic update.
- Noctua installed with the new installer keeps its data in its own Data folder next to the app.
